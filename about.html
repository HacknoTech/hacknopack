Two Decades of Corrugated Excellence

Founded in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, HACKNOPACK began as a small corrugated sheet supplier serving local manufacturers. Over 20 years, we have grown into one of the region's most trusted packaging manufacturers — with a state-of-the-art production facility, a team of experienced engineers, and a client base spanning 500+ businesses across India.

Our strength lies in our material expertise. With over 100 variants of corrugated board — spanning single, double, and triple wall constructions, multiple flute profiles, and a wide GSM range — we engineer packaging solutions that protect products through the most demanding supply chains.

From standard RSC boxes to fully custom die-cut packaging with print, we handle every order with the same commitment: strength in every layer.

Manufacturing Capabilities

Corrugated Sheet Production

High-speed corrugation lines producing single, double, and triple wall boards across all standard flute profiles (A, B, C, E, F).

Box Manufacturing

Precision die-cutting and slotting for RSC boxes, die-cut boxes, and custom structural formats to exact client specifications.

Custom Printing

Multi-colour flexographic printing directly on corrugated board — ideal for branded packaging, retail-ready boxes, and export cartons.
