Double Wall Board

Double wall corrugated board uses two fluted mediums and three flat liners, delivering significantly higher compression and puncture resistance than single wall. It is the preferred choice for industrial manufacturers, exporters, and companies shipping heavier or fragile goods.

Technical Specifications
Construction3 liners + 2 fluted mediums
Flute CombinationsBC, BE, CE flute combinations
Board Thickness6mm – 9mm
Typical GSM150–300 GSM liner
Best ForMedium to heavy weight goods
Compression StrengthHigh ECT / BCT ratings
Common Applications
Industrial machinery partsExport packagingHeavy FMCG goodsAutomotive componentsAppliance packaging
